Astronomy is my area of personal exploration. I observe the night sky, learn
about celestial objects, and document astronomical events as they happen.

What I enjoy most is that the fundamental physics is reachable. A perforated
kitchen skimmer and a sheet of cardboard are enough to project the Sun and watch
the geometry of an eclipse unfold in real time.
