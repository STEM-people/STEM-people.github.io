Planetary science is where my research work sits. I analyse data returned by
instruments flying on active missions and turn it into reconstructions of
environments that no one will ever stand in.

Most of this work is collaborative: mission data is shared, and the results only
become meaningful when they are compared, reprocessed and published openly.
