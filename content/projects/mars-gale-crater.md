# Mars Works

## The Gale Crater

I have been a member of the Mars Science Laboratory mission, to which the
Curiosity rover belongs. Using data acquired by instruments aboard this rover, I
conducted a research study that can be consulted
[here](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs17030368).

![Reconstruction of the Gale Crater environment](/planetary_sciences/mars/CuriosityVideoSeguridad.png "Screenshot from a video I produced following a reconstruction of the Gale Crater environment based on measurements obtained by the OMEGA instrument aboard the Mars Express orbiter. The reconstruction was developed using MATLAB.")
