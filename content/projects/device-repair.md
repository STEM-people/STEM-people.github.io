# Repair Works

Here are some examples of the repair services I offer. I also share tech
creations built from parts that would otherwise have been thrown away.

## Device Repair

I repair electronic devices, focusing on practical solutions and giving each
device a second life whenever that is possible. Most of the work is diagnosis:
finding out which part actually failed before replacing anything.
