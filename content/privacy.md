# Privacy

## What this site collects

Nothing is collected by the site itself. There is no contact form, no account
and no tracking beyond what is described below.

## Analytics cookies

If analytics is switched on, this site uses Google Analytics to count visits and
see which pages are read. It is only loaded **after you press Accept** on the
notice at the bottom of the screen. Until then no analytics cookie is written.

If you press Reject, nothing is loaded and the choice is remembered in your
browser. To change your mind, clear this site's data in your browser settings
and the notice will appear again.

Google Analytics records the page you visited, the approximate region you
connected from, and general information about your browser and device. It does
not receive your name or your email address.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub keeps technical
logs of requests, including IP addresses, for security and operational reasons.
That is outside the control of this site. See
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Email

If you write to the address published on this site, your message is read and
kept only for the purpose of replying to you. It is not added to any mailing
list and is not shared.

## Your rights

You can ask what data is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted,
or object to its processing, by writing to the contact address on the home page.
You also have the right to complain to the Spanish data protection authority
(Agencia Española de Protección de Datos).
